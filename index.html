Step-by-Step Feature Walkthrough

Everything you need to know about ClassFlow, straight from the interactive app tour guide.

📅 Date Navigator

Swipe left or right on the date strip to navigate between days. Tap any date to jump to it instantly, or tap 'Today' to snap back.

🎓 Class Session Cards

Each card is a class session. Tap it to mark attendance as Present, Absent, or Late — stats and ring progress update in real time.

⇄ Shift a Class

Swipe a class card left on the Home screen to reschedule it to another date and time, without touching your regular recurring timetable.

🗓️ Week Schedule View

Tap the calendar icon in the bottom nav to see your entire week at a glance with full Saturday grid mapping — perfect for spotting free slots.

📚 Course & Attendance Manager

Manage semesters and courses. Add class sessions, edit schedules, and track attendance percentage with ring UI targets per course.

✅ Tasks & Assignments

Create assignments linked to your courses, set due dates and priority tags (High, Medium, Low), with checkmark completion spring bursts.

Official Help & Guide FAQ

Authentic questions and answers sourced directly from ClassFlow's in-app Help & Guide.

How do I set up my timetable?

1. Tap the 📚 Courses tab in bottom navigation.
2. Tap ➕ to create your semester.
3. Tap + Add Class to enter course name, room, days, and time slots.
4. Your schedule appears immediately on the Home screen date strip.

How do I shift (reschedule) a class session?

Swipe a class card left on the Home screen (or tap ⇄ icon). Pick a new date, start/end time, or venue in the Shift Class dialog. A shift only moves that single session without altering your regular weekly timetable.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No! ClassFlow works completely offline. All data is stored locally on your phone using Room SQLite database. You can back up and restore via Settings → Export/Import options.

How do I mark attendance and check percentage?

Tap any class card on the Home screen to mark Present, Absent, or Late. Open Course Details to view visual attendance progress rings and target percentage indicators.
